Data validation and calculation notes

Validated CSV quoting, calendar timestamps, positive prices and sizes, known maker/taker labels, and BTCUSD symbol consistency (when the symbol column is present). Invalid rows are rejected instead of being silently classified as sell-side flow.

Gap-aware Minute-flow correlations include only exact adjacent 60-second pairs; lead/lag lookbacks require complete contiguous minutes and exact forward endpoints; compressed candles split at detected missing intervals; hourly activity uses a real time axis.

Indicators EMA overlays warm up on preceding loaded bars and restart after detected gaps. When available, the price overlay divides the sum of trade price × size by total size. Older workspaces without those sums use a clearly labelled HLC3-weighted proxy. This may differ from exchange-defined VWAP; verify the source’s size units before comparing them.

Forward samples Large-trade outcomes use the first trade at or after the target horizon only when it is within 30 seconds. Daily medians are not combined into an exact pooled median. Use per-day size distributions when an exact pooled quantile is required.

How to read this tape

Observed Price, trade size, execution timestamps, trade counts and the supplied buyer-role label.

Derived Flow signs, cumulative delta, imbalance, price bins and forward trade-price responses. These depend on the assumptions below.

  • Role-to-side assumption: buyer_role=taker is treated as buyer-initiated flow; buyer_role=maker as a sell-aggressor proxy. Verify this field's exchange-specific definition before using it for signals.
  • Size units: preserved as supplied. The file does not define the contract multiplier, so size is not relabeled as BTC or USD notional.
  • Follow-through: uses the first recorded trade at or after +1m, +5m and +15m, compared with the event's execution price. It is not a quote-mid impact measure.

What this dataset cannot reveal

There is no order-book depth, best bid/ask, spread, queue position, cancellation feed or liquidation flag. Large executions can hint at aggressive activity, but they cannot prove that resting liquidity was thin or that a stop cascade occurred.

A filename that says “monthly” does not guarantee a full month of data; check the loaded date range before analysis. The source may omit timezone metadata. Unzoned timestamps are interpreted as UTC; explicit offsets are normalized to UTC. Confirm the source’s timezone convention before interpreting session times.
